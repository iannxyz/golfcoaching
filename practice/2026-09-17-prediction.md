## Three things to hold

1. **First mistake = bogey is now the goal.** The doubles cluster rather than
   scatter — four on one nine on 07-31, three straight on 6-7-8 at Campestre.
   One bad hole recruits the next. After any tee shot in trouble, say "bogey"
   out loud before picking a club, then take the club that guarantees the next
   shot is from short grass.
2. **One more club on approach.** The measured miss is short 38 to long 10.
   Smooth 46° from 110 beats a full 50°.
3. **Chip and lag into a 3-foot circle, not at the hole.** Take whichever shot
   rolls closest — usually the 8-iron bump-and-run off a tight lie. And past the
   hole is fine; short is the problem.

If the driver misbehaves through three holes, go to the 5-wood for the day. He
shot 76 doing exactly that.
